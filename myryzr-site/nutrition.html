Log a meal in seconds — no scale, no search

Snap a photo of your plate, or type what you ate. RYZR's AI estimates the calories and macros so you can review and log — no manual database searching.

1. Snap or describe

Take a photo of your plate, or type it out — "grilled chicken breast, cup of rice, side salad with olive oil."

Some meals are easier to snap a photo of. Others are faster to type. RYZR handles both the same way — describe it, and the estimate comes back in seconds.
